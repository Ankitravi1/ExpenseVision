## 💎 Feature Tiers

### Basic (Free Forever)
*   ✅ **Unlimited Transactions**: Track as much as you want.
*   ✅ **Unlimited Accounts**: Manage checking, savings, credit cards.
*   ✅ **Core Dashboard**: Real-time insights, Net Worth tracking.
*   ✅ **Monthly Budgets**: Set limits and track progress.
*   ✅ **Manual Import/Export**: CSV support for bulk data.
*   ✅ **Dark Mode**: Beautiful glassmorphism UI.

### Pro (Premium / Kickstarter Rewards)
*   ✨ **AI Smart Import**: Scan receipts and invoices automatically.
*   ✨ **Bank Sync**: Connect directly to your bank accounts (Plaid/Teller).
*   ✨ **Advanced Analytics**: Custom date ranges, trend forecasting, category breakdown.
*   ✨ **Multi-Currency**: Auto-conversion for international travel.
*   ✨ **Budget Alerts**: Web push notifications when you near your limits.
*   ✨ **Priority Support**: Direct access to the developer.
